# Motion and editing policy

## Mode 1: KOC_REVIEW
- Make the person the anchor (default 60–80% of runtime on camera, adjustable by content).
- For concepts, use an icon plus concise annotation or a labeled side-card.
- For numeric comparison, animate validated chart axes and values with units; do not invent metrics.
- For product reviews, use closeups or product overlays; avoid altering authentic product visuals.
- Avoid random zooming and excessive transitions. Favor polished, calm accents at speech emphasis.

## Mode 2: STORYTELLING
- Map each semantic beat to a narrative function: setup, conflict, observation, realization, conclusion.
- Prefer stable identities and locations across adjacent clips, subtle parallax/push-in/pan, micro-gestures and meaningful actions.
- Abstract philosophy can be represented symbolically but make the relationship to narration clear.
- The end of a scene should lead logically to the next; avoid visual continuity jumps and implied plot events the voice never establishes.

## Shared timing, Vietnamese captions
- One canonical audio time axis in seconds. Measured voice duration determines final edit timing.
- Speech word spans must not overlap improperly; confidence below threshold is flagged for manual correction.
- Segment subtitles by natural Vietnamese phrases; typically up to 1–2 lines, responsive to phone safe area. Highlight spoken word only if actual word timestamps exist.
- Key visual onset can be a few frames before a keyword for anticipation, but the key graph number/claim must not precede its verbal context.
- Text animations: entrance 8–16 frames, hold long enough to read, exit 6–12 frames at 30fps as a starting point; adapt to pacing and audience.
- Style transitions must obey cuts and character continuity. Avoid shaking/flash effects by default.

## Checkpoints
1. Word alignment: listen to original and compare sampled timestamps.
2. Semantic correctness: graphic label/visual says what voice says, not an invented claim.
3. Data: validate all numbers, dates, signs and measurement units.
4. Accessibility: legible type, contrast, uncluttered overlays.
5. Render: frame dimensions, black frames, cut boundaries, audio peaks, outro completeness.
